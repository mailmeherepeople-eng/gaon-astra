# Learning that follows play

Source: the user's `class 6.zip`, `fees1dd/fees109.pdf` through `fees114.pdf`, NCERT *Exploring Society: India and Beyond*, Class 6. Supplied pages include 2026 reprint markings. Explanations in `lessons.js` are newly written summaries; the original game descriptions and policies remain in `index.html`.

Each note includes a village example, one recognition/recall check and a written-answer prompt with three self-marking points. Reading is not counted as successful recall. Self-marking is explicitly not machine grading. Incorrect answers cost no resources. All notes are available without grinding or tests; none are a gate to village play.

Chapter 10 notes also name constitutional/executive heads, Parliament, State assemblies and courts. Chapter 11 includes children’s participation, women’s reservation as described in the text, Hiware Bazar, PMGSY and the historical administrative ladder. Chapter 12 includes the textbook’s population bands and municipal historical examples. Chapter 14 includes the paper chain and recycling connection.

The old `SYLLABUS.md` is preserved as the **pre-Astra gap audit**; it does not describe the added journal. Its gaps that are now explained in the journal should not be mistaken for fully simulated mechanics. A journal explanation of courts does not mean the game now has a working court system.

## Validation still needed

Track voluntary returns and frustration as well as learning. The objective is self-sufficient preparation for these chapters, but passing an exam cannot be inferred from a completed note counter.
