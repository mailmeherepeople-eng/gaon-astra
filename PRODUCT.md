# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players exploring an Indian village on desktop or a phone, including students revising Class 6 civics. Phone players need to see the village and use both thumbs comfortably.

## Product Purpose

Explore Lakshmanpur, help neighbours, and earn village responsibilities. Optional journal study complements play without interrupting it.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, curious. Retain the existing paper, terracotta and village-green identity.

## Anti-references

Dashboard-like gameplay with persistent panels covering the world; desktop toolbars squeezed onto a phone.

## Design Principles

- The village and player remain the primary surface.
- Show the current objective briefly; reveal details on demand.
- Pause for reading and managing the village.
- Preserve comfortable touch controls in both orientations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable text, visible keyboard focus, scrollable dialogs, safe-area spacing and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.
