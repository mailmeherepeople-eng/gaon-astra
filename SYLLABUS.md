# Does Gaon cover the Class 6 syllabus?

Short answer: no. Gaon covers two of the book's five themes, Governance and Democracy (chapters 9 to 12) and Economic Life Around Us (chapters 13 and 14), and inside those two it covers most concepts but not all. The other three themes need different chassis, listed at the end.

Book: NCERT, *Exploring Society: India and Beyond*, Class 6 (2024). Contents page checked against the prototype and the design doc.

## Theme A, India and the World: Land and the People (chapters 1 to 3)

Locating places on the Earth (latitude, longitude, time zones, maps), oceans and continents, landforms and life. **Not covered by Gaon.** This is spatial and causal content; it wants the map chassis (point-to-point and exploration games, truth-overlay map reading) and small simulations (what a mountain does to rain and people).

## Theme B, Tapestry of the Past (chapters 4 to 6)

Timeline and sources of history, India that is Bharat, beginnings of Indian civilisation. **Not covered by Gaon.** Chapter 4 is the natural home of the timeline and card chassis (Chronology) plus a source-inspection mode; chapters 5 and 6 are timeline and map content. The Ones Who Came Before already covers chapter 4's preservation bias.

## Theme C, Our Cultural Heritage and Knowledge Traditions (chapters 7 and 8)

India's cultural roots, unity in diversity. **Not covered by Gaon.** Set-collection and pattern-building mechanics (the 10 note) fit "many in the one" better than a builder does.

## What would close the gaps

1. **Inside Gaon, for Themes D and E:** the city ages (wards, services, the three organ buildings and a court), the Centre showing up for a vast flood, a Panchayat Secretary who speeds up meetings, a paper chain (wood lot, pulp mill, printing press, school) as a late building line, and a "who is the boss" board in the Panchayat Bhavan that names President, Governor, PM and CM when petitions travel up.
2. **For the fact-shaped leftovers** (nominal heads, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha, court names, Madras 1688, Kautilya's ladder, kinship words): a card layer inside Gaon, the Chronology chassis, where petitions and events drop cards the player collects and can play later for a bonus. Facts become collectibles rather than quiz items.
3. **For Themes A, B and C:** two more games, not more Gaon. A map and expedition game for Theme A (latitude and longitude as a navigation mechanic, landforms as terrain that changes how you travel), and a timeline and sources game for Themes B and C, which Chronology and The Ones Who Came Before already sketch.

So the honest count for the whole book: Gaon can be made to cover Themes D and E nearly completely; it cannot and should not stretch to A, B and C.
